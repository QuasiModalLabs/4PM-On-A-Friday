# Business context

Read by `review-to-hardstyle` to shape the Styles prompt and set the tone of the
output. Fill this in once. Update it when something changes.

## What the business is

Danforth Fresh Market, a grocery store on the Danforth in Toronto.

## Who this is for

Internal fun. A Friday pregame, played right before the end of the shift.

Nothing here goes public, so the output can be genuinely brutal — the softening
rule for public work does not apply. It should still read as the store laughing
at itself rather than at any one customer.

## Voice

Dry and deadpan. Flat, understated, no exclamation marks.

The joke is the contrast between a completely level sentence about a grocery
store and the reverse bass underneath it. Keep the lyric phrasing plain and let
the production carry the absurdity — do not add hype, punctuation, or energy to
the reviewers' words that they did not write themselves.

## Recurring themes

Complaints:

- **Long lines.** The dominant one by a distance.
- **Self-checkout.** Unexpected item, machine freezes, waiting on an attendant
  for an override, one attendant covering every lane.

Positives:

- **Staff friendliness.** People mention the humans constantly.
- **Clean and well organised.** Tidy aisles, easy to navigate.

The queue and self-checkout material is where the raw and uptempo variants come
from. The staff and cleanliness material is what the euphoric variants get built
on — a set of five complaints alone has no dynamic range.

## Vocabulary

- **Danforth Fresh Market** — the full name. Use it where something wants to
  sound official, and in the Business line of the session file.
- **"the Fresh"** — what staff actually call it. This is the chantable one. It
  is short, it lands on a downbeat, and because it is the internal name it works
  as an in-group marker for a Friday pregame in a way the full name does not.
- **Danforth** — the street the store is on, and the neighbourhood around it.
  Keep it verbatim wherever a reviewer uses it; a specific place name delivered
  deadpan is doing most of the work in a set styled like this one. Note that a
  reviewer writing "the Danforth" may mean the street rather than the store.

Reviewers will write "Danforth Fresh Market" or "Danforth Fresh". Staff say "the
Fresh". That gap is usable — a chopped customer line that says the full name,
answered by a hook that only says "the Fresh", is the joke telling itself.

Nothing flagged to avoid yet. Add terms here as they come up.

## Off limits

**Staff names.** Always stripped and replaced with a role, per the standing rule
— this holds regardless of what else is listed here.

Nothing else is fenced off. This is internal, so a vivid complaint is good
material rather than a risk. No live legal or HR matter, no specific incident,
and no competitor restriction has been flagged. If that changes, add it here
before the next generation run — the skill cannot infer it.
